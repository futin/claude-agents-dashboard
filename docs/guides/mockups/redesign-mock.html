How to read this 6 terms ▾
Weight
The share of that hour of the week you are typically active, learned from recorded samples and folded once a week.
Evidence floor
An hour needs 30 minutes of observation before it can carry a weight at all; below that the cell is hatched, not empty.
Global mean
The average weight across every trusted cell, used for hours with no evidence of their own. Currently 0.31.
Walk
An hour-by-hour forward simulation from now to the weekly reset, adding each hour's weight × the measured burn rate.
Crossing
The first hour at which the cumulative climb touches the 100% ceiling — the projection the header quotes.
Confidence
How much of the walk rests on measured hours rather than the global mean. Below ok the header draws a band instead of a single tick.

How to read this 5 terms ▾
Weighted rate
Tokens per 1% of the 5-hour window, weighted by how much of each window that model actually held. The headline figure.
Raw tokens
Every token counted, cache reads included. Always larger than the weighted rate, and not what the limit charges.
Fitted, mixed windows
A joint fit across windows several models shared, solving for each model's own cost. A large gap against the weighted rate means the mix moved.
Baseline
The same fit over the previous fortnight. The deviation beside the headline compares the two.
Drift
The current rate has moved further from the baseline than the fit's own spread — a real price change, not sampling noise.
Local this browser
Kept in this browser's storage and never sent anywhere. A phone on the desk and the laptop each keep their own — set these again on the next device.
Shared every device
Stored by the dashboard server. One change here shows up on every device, and steers the hooks that run outside any browser.
