Sessions on a phone — Account & Board as accordions

C is the picked shape; A and B are kept for reference. Three shapes for the same idea: the two aside cards collapse to a summary row so the session list starts near the top of the screen. Tap a header to expand — the open state is the card exactly as it is today. Each frame is a real 390×760 scroll viewport — what you see is what fits before you scroll.

C · One sticky strip picked

Both collapse into a single 48 px bar that sticks under the nav: gauges left, clock + + right. Tap either half to drop its panel — the panel prints its own Account / Board title, which the strip has no room for. List starts 139 px down; the + never scrolls away.
Scroll the frame: once the strip pins it goes full-bleed — page padding cancelled, radius to 0, shadow down to a hairline. The nav auto-hides going down (as it already does), so the strip rises to the top edge and comes back under the bar on the way up.
